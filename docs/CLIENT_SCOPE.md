## Workstreams

1. DXG knowledge and pricing foundation.
2. AI-assisted RFP creation and in-build recommendations.
3. Investment guidance grounded in approved pricing.
4. Vendor proposal analysis.
5. Governance, evaluation, security, rollout, and handoff required to operate them safely.

## Acceptance principles

- Important facts and recommendations are cited or carry explicit provenance.
- Pricing is deterministic and refuses unsupported categories.
- AI output is reviewable; publication remains human-controlled.
- Client data remains tenant-isolated and private, with controlled retention and deletion.
- Provider/model releases pass the gold evaluation and client asset acceptance.
