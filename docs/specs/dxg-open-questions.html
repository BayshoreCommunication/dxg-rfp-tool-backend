Decisions

Q1 · What happens to existing users?

Decided · adds scope

DXG's decision

Keep existing users as they are, so a DXG admin can add them to an organization later. This is a new option. We don't create an organization for each user, and we don't group users by email domain.

What we'll build

At launch, existing users keep signing in and keep seeing only their own proposals, exactly as today. Behind the scenes each one gets a private holding space, so their data stays separate from every other customer. They aren't an Owner and don't get organization settings yet.

A new tool in the admin app lets DXG staff place an existing user in an organization: either a new one where they become Owner, or an existing one with a role and team. Their proposals, vendor responses and assistant history move with them, and the move is recorded in the audit log.

Q2 · How does pricing work?

Decided · adds scope

DXG's decision

Pricing is based on the number of proposals created, not on seats. For now there is a free trial that allows up to 2 proposals. Vendor responses and proposal intelligence are locked for free users.

What we'll build

There are no seat limits, so invitations are never refused for being over a limit, and the question of whether Viewers count as seats goes away. Every new organization starts on the free trial. The server enforces the 2-proposal limit and the two locked features, so the rules can't be bypassed by going around the screens.

Locked features stay visible with an upgrade prompt. The signup plan step shows the free trial. DXG staff can change an organization's plan from the admin app until paid plans and checkout are defined.

Q3 · Do Viewers need commenting?

Decided

DXG's decision

Commenting isn't needed.

What we'll build

Viewers are read-only. Commenting is dropped from the plan entirely, not deferred, and the spec's "with the ability to comment" wording is removed.

Q4 · Are templates and vendor lists shared across the organization?

Decided · adds scope

DXG's decision

Yes, shared across the organization, included at launch.

What we'll build

Today RFPilot has only two fixed presentation layouts and no saved vendor lists, so both are new features.

Organization templates: save a proposal as a reusable starting point and start new proposals from it. Owners and Admins publish templates to the whole organization. Planners can keep personal templates.

Organization vendor list: one shared list of vendor companies and contacts that anyone creating a proposal can pick from when sending it out. Owners, Admins and Team Managers edit the list.

Q5 · Authentication

Decided

DXG's decision

Go with our recommendation.

What we'll build

Keep RFPilot's current sign-in (password, Google, email codes). Add Microsoft sign-in and authenticator-app two-factor. Organizations and roles live in RFPilot. We'll revisit a hosted provider when enterprise SAML single sign-on is scheduled.

Q6 · Can Admins see planners' draft RFPs?

Decided

DXG's decision

Yes. Admins can see planners' proposals, drafts included.

What we'll build

Owners and Admins see every proposal in the organization, drafts included, as the permissions matrix says. A short note in the product tells planners that their organization's Admins can see their drafts, so nobody is surprised.

Q7 · Are Viewers inside the organization, or also outside clients?

Decided

DXG's decision

Go with our recommendation.

What we'll build

At launch, Viewers are organization members with their own login. Outside clients are invited as Viewers. Because pricing isn't per seat (Q2), this costs the customer nothing extra. Guest review links (a secure, expiring, read-only link to one proposal, with no account) come as a fast follow.

#QuestionOur assumption
F1Is the 2-proposal free limit per organization or per user?Per organization, since the organization owns the plan.
F2Does deleting a proposal free up a slot?No. The limit counts proposals created, which matches pricing by proposals created.
F3With vendor responses locked, can free users still send proposals to vendors?Yes. Vendors can respond, and responses are kept but can't be opened until the organization upgrades.
F4Do the free-plan limits apply to existing users?Not while they're unassigned. They keep today's access. Once a DXG admin places them in an organization, that organization's plan applies, and DXG sets the plan at that point.
F5What are the paid plans, and how does a customer upgrade?Until DXG defines them, the upgrade prompt says "Contact us", and DXG staff change the plan in the admin app. Online checkout is out of scope for launch.
F6Who manages shared templates and the vendor list?Owners and Admins publish organization templates. Owners, Admins and Team Managers edit the vendor list. Everyone who can create proposals can use both.
